# CombatSolver 0.50.2（fork 本地构建）

## 简体中文

- 缩减重复卡牌的选牌搜索：升级、消耗、转变等选择，以及从牌堆取牌，不再额外尝试同样卡牌的不同实体或位置。不同费用、升级和效果的卡牌仍分别考虑。
- 移除依据预测洗牌后牌序优先保留路线的策略。保留真实洗牌和后续抽牌预测，但不再为预测牌序额外保留候选。
- 这些限制会舍弃部分原本可找到的路线，可能改变战损；本次调整尚不代表求解能力已达到人类 SL 水平。

## English

- Reduce search branches for equivalent cards: upgrades, exhausts, transformations and pile retrieval no longer add alternatives for identical cards at different physical positions. Cards with different costs, upgrades or effects remain separate choices.
- Remove route retention based on the predicted order after a shuffle. Actual shuffle and subsequent draw prediction remain accurate, without reserving extra candidates for projected pile order.
- These restrictions can discard previously reachable routes and change HP loss. This change does not establish parity with human save-and-reload play.
